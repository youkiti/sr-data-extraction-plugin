## cue 01
Export writes finalized data out as three CSV formats: study_wide, results_long, and audit.
study_wide suits a Table 1 draft, results_long suits meta-analysis pre-processing in R, and audit
suits auditing or extraction-accuracy research.

## cue 02
A ready-made Methods paragraph is included too — switch language and workflow, then just copy it.

## cue 03
The preview also lists documents excluded because a final annotator couldn't be determined — here
Bergstrom 2026 is excluded since it hasn't been extracted yet.

## cue 04
If you try to generate while unverified items remain, a warning appears and you choose whether to
continue.

## cue 05
Choosing the R-ready set generates several CSVs — tab1, ma, rob and more — plus a manifest, ready to
be read directly in R.
