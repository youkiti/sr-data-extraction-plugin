## cue 01
Full extraction runs AI extraction on all remaining documents at once. All not-yet-extracted
studies are selected by default — here that's Bergstrom 2026.

## cue 02
All fields are selected by default. Pick a model and a cost estimate appears, so you can check
it before running even a large batch.

## cue 03
Pressing Run shows a confirmation dialog, and once confirmed a progress bar starts moving. Large
batches can hit the provider's rate limit (429), but picking the tier that matches your plan under
Options tunes the throttle interval and retries automatically.

## cue 04
When it finishes, the result shows the evidence count and a link straight into verification.
