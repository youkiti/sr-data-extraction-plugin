## cue 01
This is a walkthrough of the SR Data Extraction Plugin, a Chrome extension that supports
the data extraction step of a systematic review (SR).

## cue 02
It is the third tool in a trilogy: sr-query-builder for search strategies, tiab-review for
title/abstract screening, and this extension for data extraction.

## cue 03
AI drafts each value from the included papers and highlights the supporting evidence. A
human then reviews that evidence and finalizes each value with accept or edit — AI
pre-extraction paired with human verification is the core idea here.

## cue 04
In this video we'll walk through setup, creating a project, importing documents,
registering the protocol, designing the extraction schema, and running a pilot extraction.
