## cue 01
The provider isn't limited to Gemini. Switch to an OpenAI-compatible endpoint and the fields
change to an endpoint URL and an API key.

## cue 02
Setting a default reasoning effort sends it automatically to providers that support it. The
default model becomes the initial choice whenever you open a new extraction screen.

## cue 03
Under rate limiting, just pick the tier matching your plan and the request interval, concurrency,
and retries for full extraction all adjust automatically.

## cue 04
The display language can be switched here anytime, and it applies to the screen immediately.
