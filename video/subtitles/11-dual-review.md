## cue 01
Independent dual review means two reviewers extract independently, then disagreements get
adjudicated. The owner registers collaborators by email under "Reviewer management" on Home, and
picks whether each one verifies with AI visible or enters values independently without it.

## cue 02
Once both reviewers finish a study, it moves to the Adjudicate screen. Arm structures are matched
first, and if the count and names agree, they're adopted as is.

## cue 03
Matching cells get bulk-accepted, and only the disagreements are adjudicated one by one to build
the consensus, final version.

## cue 04
The inter-reviewer agreement report shows per-item agreement rate and Cohen's kappa, plus a list of
disagreeing cells.
