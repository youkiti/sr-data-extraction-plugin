## cue 01
This is the main view of the SR Data Extraction Plugin. The side navigation lists every step of the data extraction workflow, from importing documents through protocol registration, table design, pilot extraction, full extraction, verification, the dashboard, export, and adjudication.

## cue 02
The Help button in the header opens the usage guide in a new tab at any time.

## cue 03
The Settings button manages your BYOK API keys, connection method, and rate limits.
