## cue 01
Import the PDFs of your included studies — from Google Drive, or from this computer by
drag & drop or a file picker. The text layer is extracted on import.

## cue 02
Each row's text-layer column shows an ok, partial, or no_text_layer badge.

## cue 03
no_text_layer — a scanned PDF — cannot use highlight-based verification; it can only be
processed in pdf_native mode.

## cue 04
When the same trial has multiple documents, such as the main paper and a supplement, check
them and press "Merge selected studies" to combine them into one study.

## cue 05
This demo already has three fictional papers imported. The first and third are two-arm
trials and the second is a three-arm trial, showing that studies with different arm counts
can be mixed.
